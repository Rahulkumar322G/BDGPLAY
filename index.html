Games Available on BDG Play App

From colour prediction to lottery-style games, crash games and mini games — explore the game formats described on the BDG Play platform.

Win Go – Colour Prediction

Win Go is a short-round colour and number prediction game format. Players select an available option before the round ends and wait for the result. Previous results do not guarantee future outcomes.

K3 Lottery

K3 Lottery is a dice-based number game where three dice determine the result of each round. Players select their prediction before the draw ends. Review the rules and payout information before participating.

5D Lottery

5D Lottery uses five-number combinations for each round, providing different number-based prediction options. Results are chance-based, and no prediction strategy can guarantee a particular outcome.

Aviator Game

Aviator is a crash-style game in which a multiplier increases during a round. Players may choose when to cash out before the round ends. Waiting longer also increases the risk of losing the round.

TRX Hash Win

TRX Hash Win is described as a hash-based prediction game. Results are associated with transaction-hash data. Check the current platform information to understand exactly how results are generated.
